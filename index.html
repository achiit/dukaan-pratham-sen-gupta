Our story

It started at Ammi's stove.

Growing up, biryani meant Friday afternoons at home. My Ammi would start the rice and masala early, and the smell would reach the lane before lunch. Everyone who passed asked what was cooking.

We opened this cloud kitchen so Biratnagar could taste that same plate without waiting for an invitation. No dining hall, no show. Just a clean kitchen, a hot box and a rider at your door.

The logo says it simply: made with love. We try to keep that promise in every order.

  • Cooked fresh in a dedicated cloud kitchen, not reheated
  • Salad and raita packed with every single box
  • Clear weights on the menu: 500g and 700g
  • Open 11 AM to 11 PM, every day
  • Order by WhatsApp or a quick phone call
